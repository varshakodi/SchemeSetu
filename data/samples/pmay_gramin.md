# Pradhan Mantri Awaas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

## What the scheme provides

Financial assistance to eligible rural households for the construction of a
pucca house with basic amenities. The unit assistance is Rs. 1.20 lakh in
plain areas and Rs. 1.30 lakh in hilly states, difficult areas and Integrated
Action Plan districts. Funds are released in instalments linked to stages of
house construction, directly to the beneficiary's bank account.

## Who is eligible

- Houseless households, and households living in houses with kutcha walls
  and kutcha roofs, in rural areas.
- Beneficiaries are identified from housing deprivation data (SECC-based
  lists and the Awaas+ survey), verified by the Gram Sabha.
- Priority is given to the most deprived categories, including households
  with no adult member, no literate adult, and landless households deriving
  income from casual labour.

## Additional benefits (convergence)

- Support for toilet construction through Swachh Bharat Mission (Gramin).
- 90 to 95 person-days of unskilled wage employment under MGNREGA for
  house construction.
- LPG connection under Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana, and drinking water
  and electricity connections through convergence with related schemes.

## Documents required

- Aadhaar number and consent document
- Bank account details (Aadhaar-seeded)
- MGNREGA job card number, where registered
- Swachh Bharat Mission beneficiary number, where available

## How to apply

Beneficiaries do not apply directly; they are selected from the permanent
wait list generated from SECC / Awaas+ data and verified by the Gram Sabha.
Households can check their status through the local Gram Panchayat or the
PMAY-G portal (pmayg.nic.in).
