# Post Matric Scholarship for SC Students (PMS-SC)

## What the scheme provides

Financial assistance to Scheduled Caste students studying at the post-
matriculation level (class 11 and above, up to post-graduation, including
professional and technical courses). The scholarship covers an academic
allowance (which includes maintenance) and compulsory non-refundable fees
charged by the institution.

## Who is eligible

- The student belongs to a Scheduled Caste (a caste certificate is required).
- The student is studying in class 11 or above at a recognised institution.
- The combined annual income of the student's parents or guardians does not
  exceed Rs. 2.5 lakh.
- Only two male children of the same parents are eligible; there is no such
  restriction for female children.

## Documents required

- Caste certificate issued by a competent authority
- Income certificate of parents/guardians
- Previous year's marksheet
- Aadhaar number and Aadhaar-seeded bank account
- Fee receipt and admission proof from the institution

## How to apply

Apply online through the National Scholarship Portal (scholarships.gov.in)
or the relevant state scholarship portal within the notified application
window each academic year. The institution and the district welfare office
verify the application before the scholarship is sanctioned.
