# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

## What the scheme provides

Income support of Rs. 6,000 per year to eligible landholding farmer families,
paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 every four months. The money is
transferred directly into the beneficiary's bank account through Direct
Benefit Transfer (DBT).

## Who is eligible

- All landholding farmer families that have cultivable land registered in
  their names, subject to the exclusion criteria below.
- A "family" for this scheme means husband, wife and minor children.
- State governments identify eligible families using land ownership records.

## Who is NOT eligible (exclusions)

- Institutional landholders.
- Present or former holders of constitutional posts.
- Present or former ministers, MPs, MLAs, mayors and district panchayat chairpersons.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of central or state government
  departments and PSUs (excluding Multi-Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
- Anyone who paid income tax in the last assessment year.
- Practising professionals such as doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered
  accountants and architects.
- Pensioners drawing a monthly pension of Rs. 10,000 or more (excluding
  MTS / Class IV / Group D).

## Documents required

- Aadhaar card (mandatory; eKYC must be completed)
- Land ownership records
- Bank account details or passbook

## How to apply

Register through the PM-KISAN portal (pmkisan.gov.in), a Common Service
Centre (CSC), or the state nodal officers. Completing eKYC (OTP-based or
biometric) is mandatory for instalments to be released.
